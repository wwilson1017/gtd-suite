Refunds

Last updated November 14, 2026

Ask within 14 days of any charge and we refund it in full, no questions asked.

How to get a refund

  • Email support@tncheesecake.com from the email on your account, or
  • contact Link support, who sold you Pro on our behalf.

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with. The bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 days.

What a refund does

When we refund a charge we also cancel the subscription, so you aren't charged again, unless you tell us you'd like to keep Pro. The account goes back to the free limits; nothing you made is deleted.

After 14 days

Cancel any time in Settings → Plan → Manage billing, and you won't be charged again. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. We don't usually refund part of a period after 14 days, but write to us if something went wrong and we'll look at it. Stripe may also refund a charge within 60 days in some cases, and in the EU and UK you always have at least 14 days to change your mind.
