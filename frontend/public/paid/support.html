Support

Help with Todo GTD, Projects GTD, Notes GTD, Simple Sites and Common Brain

Email support@tncheesecake.com
We answer within two business days, usually sooner. Please send it from the email on your account, and say which app you were using.

Billing and Pro

  • Cancel, switch monthly ↔ yearly, or change your card: in any app, Connect → Manage billing.
  • Receipts: sent by email from Link when you pay. Lost one? Look up your order at link.com, or ask us.
  • Refunds: see Refunds.
  • Pro is sold through Link, Stripe's merchant of record, so you can also go to Link support about a payment.

Common questions

  • I lost my links. If your email is confirmed, use "Sign in" on any app's front page and we'll email you a sign-in link.
  • Someone else has my link. Replace your links under Connect: the old ones stop working at once.
  • My AI tool can't connect. Open Connect in the app and follow the steps for your tool, or email us with the tool's name and what it says.
  • Report a page. Email us the page's address and what's wrong with it.
