Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Last updated November 22, 2026

In short: we keep what you put in the apps so you can use it, and nothing else. No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your data. We don't run AI on your content; the AI tools you connect do, under their own policies.

Who is responsible

Tennessee Cheesecake Inc., 1420 Toshiba Drive, STE B, Lebanon, TN 37087, USA, runs the GTD suite and is responsible for your data. Questions or requests: support@tncheesecake.com.

What we keep

  • What you make: todos, projects, notes, boards, Simple Sites pages and links, Common Brain memories, pages and files, and who wrote each one (you, a person you added, or the name of the AI tool).
  • Your email, if you add one: to confirm it, send sign-in links, and send receipts and account notices. No newsletters unless you ask for them.
  • Your time zone, so "today" means your today.
  • Pro: your Stripe customer number and the state of your subscription. Card details go to Stripe and Link, never to us.
  • Technical data: your IP address and the pages you request, in server logs kept for a short time to run the service and stop abuse. Those logs can include the addresses you open, so they stay private and are looked at only to fix problems. Simple Sites counts how often each page is opened, with no record of who opened it.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in on a browser (up to a year, until you sign out). A password-protected Simple Sites page sets one more, for 30 days, once you've typed its password. Your browser also remembers small settings, like light or dark mode. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers.

Who else handles your data

Only the services the apps need to run, each bound to protect it:

  • Railway runs the apps and the database, in Virginia, United States.
  • Cloudflare stores Common Brain's files (R2) and runs our domain names.
  • Resend sends sign-in and confirmation emails.
  • Stripe and Link take Pro payments, as merchant of record, under Stripe's privacy policy.
  • AI tools you connect (Claude, ChatGPT and others) read and write what you let them, under their own privacy policies. You choose them, and you can cut one off by replacing your agent link.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI. We disclose it only if the law requires it.

Simple Sites visitors

A Simple Sites page is public to anyone with its address. If a page collects what visitors type into it, the person who made the page decides what is collected and is responsible for it; we only store it for them. Visitors with questions about such a page should contact its owner, or us if they can't.

How long we keep it

  • Your content stays until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Most items are archived rather than deleted, so you can bring them back.
  • Backups: copies of the database are taken every hour and kept for up to a year, on our own backup machines in the United States. A deleted account disappears from backups as they age out.
  • An account never used at all is deleted after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and copy: everything you made is in the apps. Common Brain exports it as files, and we'll send a full copy of any account on request.
  • Correct or delete: edit anything in the apps; delete Simple Sites pages and Common Brain items for good yourself; email support to delete your whole account.
  • Wherever you live, including the EU, UK and California, you can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. We answer within 30 days.

Children

The apps aren't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data about them. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll email confirmed accounts before it applies.
